Full eight-direction actor sets:

- `crypt_guard`: armored melee undead
- `bone_acolyte`: ranged/caster undead

Bone Acolyte currently maps its authoritative ranged attack to the generic
`Attack` actor state. A future content/runtime revision may add monster-specific
`Cast` use without changing this manifest registry architecture.
