# V36.90 Player Equipment Layers

Local player equipment is rendered as synchronized transparent actor layers.
The authoritative equipped_slot selects apparel layers; distance_weapon selects
melee/ranged weapon art and light_source selects guard/light offhand art.

All layers mirror the body Idle/Walk/Attack/Hit/Death/Cast/Use frame contract.
Remote player gear is intentionally unchanged because remote PlayerView does
not currently carry equipped inventory.
